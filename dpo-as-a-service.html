The Problem

Why Most Indian Companies Are Exposed

The DPDP Act 2023 is enforceable now. Most businesses don't have the expertise or the budget to handle compliance in-house.

⚠️
CRITICAL RISK

No Qualified DPO on Staff

The DPDP Act requires organisations to appoint a qualified Data Protection Officer. Most mid-market companies have no one with the required expertise — and hiring takes months.

💸
COST BARRIER

Full-Time DPO Is Unaffordable

A certified DPO commands ₹30–60 LPA in India. For startups and SMEs, this is simply out of reach — creating a compliance gap that regulators will not ignore.

📋
COMPLEXITY

DPDP Compliance Is Not a One-Time Fix

Consent management, DSR handling, ROPA maintenance, breach notifications, vendor assessments — ongoing compliance requires dedicated, expert attention every single month.

Our Services

Everything a DPO Does — Delivered as a Service

DPO Appointment & Registration

Policy & Notice Drafting

ROPA & Data Mapping

Pricing Factors

What Determines Your Plan

Every business is different. These five factors help us size the right DPO engagement for your organisation.

🏢

Company Size

Number of employees and the volume of personal data processed each month.

🏭

Industry & Risk Level

Regulated sectors (BFSI, healthcare) require more intensive oversight and documentation.

📊

Data Complexity

Number of processing activities, data categories (including sensitive data), and third-party vendors.

📮

Expected DSR Volume

Monthly volume of data subject requests — access, erasure, correction — drives service scope.

🌐

Cross-Border Transfers

International data flows and multi-entity structures add compliance scope and reporting requirements.
