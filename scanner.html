What does this scanner check?

TrustFabric's free DPDP cookie scanner analyses any website for cookie and consent violations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. It detects trackers loaded before consent, missing or weak consent banners, third-party cookies, and gaps between your privacy policy and actual cookie behaviour.

Who needs a DPDP cookie compliance check?

Any website that collects personal data from users in India — including e-commerce stores, SaaS platforms, news websites, and enterprise portals — must comply with the DPDP Act 2023. A cookie compliance check is the first step to understanding your exposure and fixing consent management gaps before enforcement begins.

How does the scanner work?

Enter your website URL and our scanner visits your site just like a real user — capturing all cookies and network requests before and after consent is given. It then maps these against DPDP Act requirements and generates a detailed compliance report you can download instantly.
