About TrustFabric

Building a more trusted digital world.

Security and privacy should never be afterthoughts. We help organisations understand their data, protect what matters and innovate responsibly.

Technology is moving forward. Trust must move with it.

Businesses collect, process and use more data than ever before. Every new system, integration and AI tool adds to it, and with it, the responsibility to protect it.

  • Data lives in many places.

    Organisations often do not know where their sensitive data resides, which makes it hard to protect or even to account for.

  • Privacy is now an obligation.

    Laws such as India's DPDP Act 2023 turn responsible data handling from a good intention into a duty.

  • Old approaches are falling behind.

    Traditional security approaches struggle to keep pace with how quickly technology and threats evolve.

  • AI cuts both ways.

    It opens up new opportunities and introduces new security risks, which need to be understood early.

TrustFabric exists to help organisations meet these challenges with practical, technology-driven solutions.

What we are building.

A growing set of technologies, built on one idea: to protect what matters, first understand it.

Data Privacy and Consent Management

A consent management platform designed around India's DPDP Act, helping organisations collect, record and honour the choices people make about their data.

Explore the platform

Data Discovery and Classification

You cannot protect data you cannot find. We are building tools to discover, understand and classify personal data across an organisation.

AI Security and Penetration Testing

AI brings new capability and new risk. This is a growing focus for us: understanding how AI systems can be attacked, and how to secure them.

Enterprise Cybersecurity

Privacy and security are two halves of the same problem. We bring them together to help organisations protect the data that matters most.
