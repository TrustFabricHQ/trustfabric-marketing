01

Day 01

Understand the framework

Dates announced soon

  1. 01DPDP Act 2023 and the DPDP Rules 2025: scope, definitions and obligations

    How the Act and Rules apply, the key terms they define, and what they require of organisations that handle personal data.

  2. 02Data Principal rights and how to handle them

    The rights the Act gives individuals over their data, and how an organisation can receive, track and respond to those requests.

  3. 03The role of the Data Protection Officer

    What a DPO is responsible for, and how the role fits within an organisation’s privacy programme.

  4. 04Lawful basis for processing personal data

    The grounds on which personal data can be processed, and how to document the basis you rely on.

  5. 05Data Fiduciary versus Data Processor

    How the two roles differ, who carries which obligations, and why it matters for contracts and vendors.

  6. 06Enterprise privacy framework and security controls

    How policies, processes and technical safeguards come together into a working privacy framework.

02

Day 02

Put it into practice

Dates announced soon

  1. 01Breach management and the Data Protection Board

    How to prepare for, respond to and report a personal data breach, and where the Data Protection Board fits in.

  2. 02Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)

    How to assess and document the privacy risks of a processing activity before it begins.

  3. 03Consent and notice, including parental consent

    What a clear notice and valid consent request look like, and the extra care needed when children’s data is involved.

  4. 04Data Processing Agreements

    What to cover when a third party processes personal data on your behalf.

  5. 05Hands-on lab: build a RoPA, run a DPIA, check a website’s privacy posture

    Put the learning to work on practical exercises: a record of processing activities, an impact assessment and a website privacy check.

  6. 06Final assessment

    A closing assessment that completes the programme.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any prior experience?

No. The course starts from the fundamentals of the DPDP Act and builds up. If you already work in privacy, legal or IT, the labs on Day 2 let you go deeper on your own scenarios.

Will I get access to the recordings?

Yes. Recording links are shared by email after the sessions end, so you can revisit any module.

How does the certificate work?

Participants who complete both days and the assessment receive the CDPP certificate as a PDF by email. Each one has a unique certificate ID and is issued by TrustFabric.

What is the refund policy?

Refund and transfer terms for this batch will be confirmed before registration opens. For questions in the meantime, write to info@trustfabric.in.

Can I get a GST invoice?

Yes. A GST invoice is available for every registration. If you need it issued to your company, write to us with your company name and GSTIN after enrolling.

What do I need to join?

A laptop or desktop with a stable internet connection and Zoom or Google Meet. The joining link is emailed to you after you enroll.
